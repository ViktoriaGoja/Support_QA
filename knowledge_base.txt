CONNECTICUT LEARNER'S PERMIT — HOW TO GET YOUR PERMIT
================================================================================

AGE REQUIREMENT:
- You must be at least 16 years old to get your learner's permit in Connecticut.

BEFORE THE PERMIT TEST — WORK ZONE SAFETY COURSE:
- Connecticut requires all new drivers to complete an online Work Zone Safety
  Training Program BEFORE taking the permit test.
- You must show the completion certificate on permit test day or you will not
  be permitted to test.

THE PERMIT TEST:
- 25 multiple choice questions, randomly selected from a pool of 300 questions.
- You must get at least 20 correct to pass (no more than 5 wrong).
- A vision test is also required. Bring glasses/contacts if you wear them.
- Available in multiple languages: English, Spanish, Polish, French, Italian,
  Portuguese, Mandarin Chinese, Arabic, Russian, Vietnamese, and Albanian.
- Audio headset testing accommodations are available.
- You may use a non-electric dictionary for translation (no additional markings
  or papers inside).

PERMIT TEST COST:
- Total fee: $59, paid at the time the permit is issued.
  - $19 permit fee + $40 testing fee.
  - The $40 testing fee covers your first road test.

HOW TO SCHEDULE THE PERMIT TEST:
- All tests must be completed in person at a CT DMV branch.
- Permit testing is by appointment only.
- Schedule online at: https://dmvteen.ct.gov/dmv/do-it-online
- DMV locations offering permit tests: Bridgeport, Cheshire, Danbury, Enfield,
  Hamden, Norwalk, Old Saybrook, Waterbury, Wethersfield, and Willimantic.

WHAT TO BRING TO THE DMV FOR YOUR PERMIT TEST:
- Completed R-229 application form (non-commercial learner permit/driver's license)
- Work Zone Safety Course completion certificate
- 2 documents proving identity (U.S. birth certificate, U.S. passport, or
  passport card)
- 2 documents proving Connecticut residency (bank statement, pre-printed pay
  stub, or postmarked mail)
- 1 document showing Social Security Number (Social Security card, W-2 form,
  or 1099 issued within past 5 years)
- Glasses or contacts if you wear them (for vision test)
- $59 fee (paid upon passing)

FREE PERMIT PRACTICE TEST:
- The Next Street offers a free online permit practice test at:
  https://www.thenextstreet.com/permit-practice-test
- Generates a new 25-question test every time you take it. Keep refreshing
  to practice with different questions.
- A free Permit Prep eBook is also available for studying.

DO I NEED MY PERMIT TO START DRIVER'S ED?
- No! You do NOT need your learner's permit to start classroom courses.
- However, you WILL need a valid permit before scheduling driving lessons
  or a road test.

TEMPORARY PERMITS & PHOTO REQUIREMENTS (The Next Street Policy):
- When you pass your permit test at the DMV, you receive a paper temporary
  permit for immediate use. Your permanent photo permit arrives by mail
  within approximately 20 business days.
- IMPORTANT: The Next Street DOES NOT allow students to take driving lessons
  with a temporary permit that does not have their photo on it. You must wait
  for your permanent photo permit to arrive before scheduling lessons.
- Track your permit delivery at the CT DMV customer center online.
Reference: https://portal.ct.gov/dmv/licenses-permits-ids/get-learners-permit

QUALIFIED TRAINER REQUIREMENTS (for practice driving with permit):

- Eligible trainers: spouse, parent, grandparent, foster parent, legal

CT PERMITS, LICENSES & STATE REGULATIONS
================================================================================

--- ADULT LEARNER'S PERMIT (Age 18+) ---
- Must be 18 or older
- Must pass vision test and 25-question knowledge test at DMV
- Must hold permit for minimum 90 days before road test
- Must complete 8-hour Safe Driving Practices Course at a licensed school
- Fees: $40 testing fee + $19 permit fee = $59 total
- 90-DAY HOLD EXEMPTIONS: Active-duty military stationed out of state,
  previous CT permit/license holders, or those with prior out-of-state
  driving experience (must provide expired license or driving history)
Reference: https://portal.ct.gov/dmv/licenses-permits-ids/get-learners-permit/adult-learners-permit

--- TEEN DRIVER'S LICENSE (Ages 16-17) ---
- Step 1: Get learner's permit (age 16+, pass vision + knowledge test)
- Step 2: Complete driver training (120-180 days with permit):
  - Commercial school (like The Next Street): 30 hours classroom + 40 hours
    practice driving (2 hours with instructor count toward the 40)
  - Home training with qualified trainer: 22 hours classroom + 40 hours practice
- Step 3: Road test (eligible after 120 days with school training, 180 days
  with home training)
- Must present permit, completed application, and eDec certificate at road test
- After passing road test: Wait 48 hours before visiting DMV office for license
- Retest waiting period: 14 days; Retest fee: $40
- Parent/guardian of 16-17 year old must attend a 2-hour training course
- Passenger restrictions and curfew laws apply to newly licensed 16-17 year olds
- Extended curfew exception: driving for school or religious activities
Reference: https://portal.ct.gov/dmv/licenses-permits-ids/teen-drivers-license

--- OUT-OF-STATE LICENSE TRANSFER ---
- New CT residents have 90 days to transfer their out-of-state license
- Requirements: current out-of-state license (not expired more than 2 years),
  vision test at DMV, Form R-229, acceptable ID documents
- Fees: $84 license fee + $40 application fee = $124 total
- License arrives by mail within 20 business days
- Out-of-state learner's permits are NOT transferable to CT — you must apply
  for a new CT learner's permit
- WHO DOES NOT NEED TO TRANSFER:
  - Full-time students maintaining out-of-state permanent residence
  - Active-duty military and dependents
  - Visitors on visa status (may drive up to 1 year with valid foreign license + IDP)
- EXPIRED LICENSE (over 2 years): Must complete CT learner's permit process
  and 8-hour Safe Driving Course before road test
- UNDER 18: Must complete 8-hour safe driving class + 2-hour parent training;
  subject to passenger restrictions
- UNDER 16: Cannot transfer license to CT
Reference: https://portal.ct.gov/dmv/licenses-permits-ids/transfer-out-of-state-license

--- INTERNATIONAL DRIVER PERMITS (IDP) ---
- An IDP is an English translation of a foreign-language license issued by
  a country outside the U.S.
- An IDP alone is NOT a valid license — it must accompany the original
  valid foreign license at all times
- Students/visitors with a valid foreign license may drive in Connecticut
  for up to 1 year with their foreign license (IDP required if license is
  not in English or Spanish)
- The IDP can ONLY be obtained in the country that issued the original license
- After 1 year, you must obtain a CT driver's license
- Out-of-country licenses are NOT acceptable as a form of identification
  for DMV transactions
- CT residents traveling abroad: obtain an IDP through AAA (Automobile
  Association of America)
- THE NEXT STREET POLICY: Students with an international license/IDP are
  not eligible for road testing at The Next Street. They should consult
  with the DMV about their specific licensing path.
Reference: https://portal.ct.gov/DMV/Licenses/Licenses/International-Driver-Permits

--- NON-U.S. CITIZEN DRIVER'S LICENSE ---
- Non-U.S. citizens seeking a first-time CT permit or license MUST go to
  a DMV hub office (not a branch) to apply in person
- Must prove: identity, legal presence in the U.S., and CT residency
- Immigration status verified through SAVE program — may take 10+ business
  days (plan ahead!)
- Required documents:
  - Foreign passport with supporting immigration documents
  - I-94 (hard copy from US Customs and Border Protection website)
  - Social Security Number proof
  - 2 pieces of CT mail (within 90 days) proving residency
- Non-English documents require translation by DMV-approved translator
- Only U.S. citizens and permanent residents are eligible for REAL ID
- DACA/DREAM Act applicants: May qualify for non-verified driver's license
  using I-94 or Employment Authorization Card
Reference: https://portal.ct.gov/DMV/Licenses/Licenses/Drivers-License--NonUS-Citizen--Requirements

--- DRIVE-ONLY LICENSE ---
- Designed for undocumented individuals age 16+ who cannot establish lawful
  U.S. presence
- NOT valid for federal identification or voting
- May be honored in other states — verify before traveling
- NOT ELIGIBLE if you have a felony conviction in CT or committed identity
  fraud at CT DMV
- Process: documents → knowledge + vision test → learner's permit by mail →
  driver training → road test → drive-only license by mail
- Fees: $40 testing + $19 permit + $36 license (3-year validity)
- DMV conducts criminal background checks
- Must sign affidavit committing to file immigration legalization
  applications when eligible
- Drive-only licenses CANNOT be transferred from another state — must apply
  in CT
- THE NEXT STREET POLICY: Students with a drive-only learner's permit follow
  the same training path as standard permit holders. The drive-only permit
  must have a photo to schedule lessons.
Reference: https://portal.ct.gov/dmv/licenses-permits-ids/get-drive-only-license

CT DRIVING LESSON SYLLABUS (What to Expect at Each Lesson)
================================================================================

General Notes:
- Lessons are sold individually or as part of a package. Not all students will
  have 8 hours of lessons. You can buy as few or as many as you need.
- We recommend 8 hours of lessons for the average new driver.
- Students ages 16-17 can only take 2 hours of driving lessons per day.
- Every new driver develops skills at different rates. Instructors prioritize
  safety and mastery of basics before moving to advanced skills.
- Practice at home between lessons is very important (minimum 10 hours between
  each lesson, 30-minute sessions or more).
- Before Lesson 1: We recommend students have at least basic understanding of
  a car and its controls. Best if they have driven in a parking lot or on
  secondary roads first. If no access to a vehicle, let instructor know.

WHAT TO BRING TO EVERY LESSON:
- Physical permit (no copies or digital versions)
- Glasses/contacts if required by B restriction
- Proper footwear (sneakers recommended, no open-toe, heels, crocs, or work boots)
- Evaluation sheet from previous lesson (lessons 2+)

LESSON 1 (2 hours):
Skills covered: Pre-trip inspection, dashboard introduction, instructor brake
explanation, parking maneuvers, blind spot checks, steering, lane tracking,
cornering, signal use, back-up posture, 3-point turn, pull-in parking (left
and right). Most of first lesson is in parking lot with limited backroad driving.
Last 10 minutes: Instructor reviews with student and parent, fills out evaluation.
Note: Parallel parking is NOT normally taught on Lesson 1.
Homework: Practice minimum 10 hours before Lesson 2 (parking, braking,
accelerating, cornering, secondary roads, scanning).

LESSON 2 (2 hours):
Skills covered: Pre-trip inspection, more parking, urban roads, lane changes,
parallel parking (if skill level allows).
Homework: Practice minimum 10 hours before Lesson 3. Student should have 15-20
hours total practice before Lesson 3 (highway driving).

LESSON 3 (2 hours):
Skills covered: Pre-trip inspection, lane changes, blind spot review, parking,
traffic controls, space and speed management, highway driving (entering, yielding,
lane changes, lane uses, exiting), parallel parking if time allows.
Homework: Practice minimum 10 hours before Lesson 4 (all skills including highway).

LESSON 4 (2 hours):
Skills covered: Pre-trip inspection, skills needing more practice, general driving,
lights, intersections, stop signs, parallel parking. This is a comprehensive
review and readiness assessment.
Last 10 minutes: Instructor reviews and indicates if student needs more time
before testing.
Homework: Practice minimum 10 hours before test date.

LESSON 5 — MOCK TEST (2 hours, sold separately):
This is a special mock test option, NOT included in the Full Driver Education
Bundle. Purchased separately in Student Portal.
Format: Instructor acts as DMV agent administering an official-style license test.
Each mock test takes 15-20 minutes. Instructor does NOT actively instruct during
the mock test. After each test, instructor reviews evaluation for mistakes and
runs additional mock tests for remaining time.

OUR FLEET:
All lessons are in Next Street dual-controlled vehicles. Current fleet includes:
2021 Hyundai Accent, 2021 Hyundai Elantra, 2021 Nissan Sentra, 2022 Kia Forte,
2020 Toyota Corolla, 2022 Nissan Sentra, 2021 Toyota Prius, 2022 Toyota Prius,
2023 Toyota Corolla Hybrid, 2024 Toyota Corolla Hybrid, 2024 Toyota Prius.
We diversify our fleet — specific vehicle requests are not allowed.
All vehicles have a second brake on the passenger side for instructor safety control.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: How much does it cost?
A: Our pricing varies by product and location. For the most up-to-date pricing,
please visit our enrollment page at https://www.thenextstreet.com or contact us
at help@thenextstreet.com. Prices are subject to change.

Q: What are your hours?
A: Driving lessons are available 7 days a week, 8 AM to 9 PM. Classroom sessions
have various scheduling options — check the Student Portal for current availability.
Road tests at The Next Street run Monday-Friday, 8 AM to 2 PM (CT) or as
scheduled by the RMV (MA). Our Customer Support Center is also available to help.

Q: How do I sign up / enroll?
A: Visit https://www.thenextstreet.com to enroll online. All scheduling and
progress tracking are managed through your Student Portal after enrollment.

Q: Do my services expire?
A: Yes. All services expire 2 years from the date of enrollment. If you do not
complete your services within that time, they are forfeited and you would need
to re-enroll. We recommend not enrolling until you are eligible to receive credit
for the service to make the most of your 2-year window.

Q: Do I need a learner's permit to start?
A: You do NOT need a permit to start classroom courses. However, you WILL need
a valid learner's permit before you can schedule driving lessons or a road test.

Q: How do I schedule lessons?
A: All scheduling is done online through your Student Portal. New lesson slots
open every Thursday evening for the next 60 days. We do not book out longer
than 60 days. For best availability, check Thursday nights or join the Wait List.

Q: Can you pick me up for lessons?
A: No. All lessons start and end at a Next Street location. We do not offer
home pickup or drop-off.

Q: How far in advance should I book?
A: Lessons often book 4-6 weeks in advance. We recommend checking the Student
Portal Thursday evenings when new slots open for the best availability.

Q: What car do you use for lessons?
A: All lessons are conducted in Next Street dual-controlled vehicles equipped
with GPS tracking and video recording. We do not train in personal vehicles.

Q: What language are lessons taught in?
A: All instruction is in English only. Zoom classroom sessions offer live
closed-caption translation for accessibility.

Q: What is the refund policy?
A: Individual products purchased within the last 6 months are eligible for a
refund, subject to a 10% processing fee. Bundle purchases are non-refundable
once any service in the bundle has been started.

Q: What is an eDec?
A: An eDec is Connecticut's digital completion certificate. It's required before
you can take your road test. We issue it in your Student Portal once all
requirements (sessions, quizzes, attendance forms) are complete. Complete
everything at least 5 days before your scheduled test date. If you are testing
at The Next Street, your eDec is uploaded to your Student Portal the day before
your test. You do NOT need to print it — it is sent electronically to the DMV.
IMPORTANT: We cannot issue your eDec without a valid permit number on file.
Please make sure to update your permit number in your Student Portal as soon
as you receive your permit.

Q: What is a Driver Education Certificate?
A: In Massachusetts, it's the official RMV certificate issued after completing
all driver education requirements (classroom + driving + observation for teens).
The Next Street submits your results electronically to the RMV, and the
certificate is issued in the RMV's Atlas system.

Q: How long do I have to wait after getting my permit?
A: Massachusetts: Teens must hold permit for at least 6 months. Adults have no
mandatory wait.
Connecticut: Adults must wait 90 days. Teens must wait 180 days (or 120 days
if they completed the Full Driver Education Bundle with 30-hour course + 8 hours
driving lessons).

Q: Are RMV/DMV testing fees included?
A: No. RMV (MA) and DMV (CT) testing fees are paid directly to the respective
agency and are not included in any Next Street product.

Q: Can I take my road test at The Next Street?
A: Yes! In both MA and CT, we offer road testing at our locations. The RMV/DMV
examiner comes to us. You test in our car, in a calm familiar environment.
You must complete at least one 2-hour driving lesson with us first.

Q: I want to talk to someone / speak to a person.
A: You can reach our team at help@thenextstreet.com or call our Customer Support
Center. We're happy to help!

Q: Where are your locations?
A: The Next Street operates multiple locations across Massachusetts and Connecticut.
For a complete list of locations with addresses, hours, and directions, visit
https://www.thenextstreet.com/locations. Locations may change, so always check
the website for the most current information.
